One suggestion that will make your platform look even more premium

Instead of calling it a "Crypto Trading Platform," brand it as an "AI Digital Asset Management Platform."

That subtly changes how people perceive it. "Trading platform" sounds like a place to gamble or manually trade. "Digital Asset Management Platform" sounds like a sophisticated financial technology company that uses AI to help users manage their crypto investments. It tends to inspire more confidence while still accurately describing the service you want to provide.
